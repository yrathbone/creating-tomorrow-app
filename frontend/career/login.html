Your Career Profile belongs to you

Creating Tomorrow can securely save the career information you choose to confirm so you don't have to rebuild your history every time you apply.

  • Your Career Profile information will be stored for future use.
  • AI may suggest career information, but suggestions do not become verified evidence until you confirm them.
  • Creating Tomorrow will not invent career experience.
  • You can review and edit your stored information.
  • You will be able to delete your Career Profile and account data.
  • The existing anonymous tools remain separate from your saved Career Profile.
  • Original uploaded resume files are not retained after we extract information from them.
