Creating Tomorrow's Career Profile stores your work history, skills, and education — your name, job titles, employers, dates, and the accomplishments you describe — so tools like Right Fit can compare against your whole career, not just one uploaded resume.

Nothing is added to your profile without your explicit confirmation — not even something an AI suggests.

You'll be able to review, edit, export, or delete this information later.
