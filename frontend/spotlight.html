What does your LinkedIn profile say about you?

Your experience already tells a story. Let's make sure your profile makes that story easy to understand.

Guide can review the information you choose to share and help you see what's already working, what could be clearer, and where your experience may be getting overlooked.

Your profile belongs to you. Only upload your own profile or information you have permission to use. You can remove personal information you don't want analyzed. We don't save your screenshots, PDF, or profile text — they're sent securely to our AI provider to generate your review, and are typically deleted from their systems within 30 days.
