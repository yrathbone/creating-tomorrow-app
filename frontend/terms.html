Terms of use

Simple ground rules for using Creating Tomorrow.

Last updated October 8, 2026

Creating Tomorrow is a free career-education project. By using this site you agree to the points below. If you don’t agree, please don’t use it.

What the tools are, and aren’t

  • The tools and guides are for general career education. They aren’t professional, legal or employment advice.
  • Results are suggestions. We can’t promise an interview, a job, or a particular score in any hiring system.
  • AI can be wrong or miss things. Read everything before you use it.

Your part

  • You’re responsible for what you submit and for the resumes and profiles you send to employers. Make sure they’re accurate. Creating Tomorrow is built to help you describe real experience, not to invent it, and you agree not to use it to make false claims.
  • Only submit information you have the right to share. Please don’t include other people’s private information.
  • Don’t misuse the site: no attempts to break it, overload it, scrape it, or get into anyone else’s account.

Your content

What you write and upload stays yours. You let us process it to produce your results and, if you have a Career Profile, to save it for you, as described on the Privacy page.

Accounts

A Career Profile is optional. Keep your sign-in details to yourself. We may limit or close accounts that are misused, and you can delete yours yourself from your dashboard at any time, or ask us to.

No guarantees

The site is free and provided “as is.” It may change, be interrupted, or contain errors, and to the extent the law allows, Creating Tomorrow isn’t liable for losses that come from using it.

Changes and questions
